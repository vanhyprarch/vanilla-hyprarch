Universal clipboard shortcut callbacks in bindings.lua are adapted from Omarchy:
https://github.com/omacom/omarchy
Commit: 51bfd1dbaa1b5f790e19623faf2745945d40c796
Source files:
  default/hypr/bindings/clipboard.lua
  default/hypr/apps/terminals.lua

Vanilla uses Omarchy's 50 ms native key-release timer and generic terminal
identifiers, with exact class/initial_class matching. Foot's server-mode app-id
and WezTerm's documented default app-id are additional verified identifiers;
see docs/system-baseline.md for their sources. Omarchy-specific application IDs,
TUI handling, terminal tags, and unrelated bindings are not included.
